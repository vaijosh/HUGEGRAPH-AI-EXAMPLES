These documents are U.S. Department of Justice press releases, retrieved on 2026-10-23 from the public
DOJ press-release API (https://www.justice.gov/api/v1/press_releases.json). Works of the U.S. federal
government are in the public domain. Text was converted from HTML to plain text; images and standard
closing boilerplate (presumption of innocence, maximum-penalty statements, contact details) were removed.
sources.csv lists the original URL, office and date of every file.

The releases name real people. Charged defendants are presumed innocent unless and until proven guilty;
treat this folder as demo data only and do not publish graphs built from it as statements of fact.
