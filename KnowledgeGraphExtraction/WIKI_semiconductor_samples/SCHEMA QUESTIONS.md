# Schema questions (semiconductor)

Paste the lines in the block below into **Questions this graph should answer** before clicking *Infer schema*,
and leave them there for the ingest: they steer both schema inference and extraction.
They say what the graph must answer, so the inferred schema keeps the relationships and numbers the questions in
`SEMICONDUCTOR QUESTIONS to ASK.md` need. They are general on purpose, so they do not copy the test questions.

```text
Who founded each company, in which year, and who is or has been its CEO?
Where is each company headquartered (city, state or province, and country)?
Which products does each company develop, and what type of product is each?
Which companies did each company acquire, in which year, and for how much?
Which companies invested in each company?
Which companies compete with each other?
```
