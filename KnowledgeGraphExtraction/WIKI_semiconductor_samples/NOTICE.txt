These files are excerpts (the opening sections, about 5,000 characters each) of English Wikipedia articles,
retrieved on 2026-10-26 through the MediaWiki API as plain text. Wikipedia text is licensed under the Creative
Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 license (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/); the authors are the
Wikipedia contributors listed in each article's history. sources.csv gives each article's URL. The excerpts are
unmodified apart from the Title / Source header and the removal of section breaks.

Use them as a second, non-legal test dataset for graphrag_showcase.py: companies, founders and executives,
headquarters, products and acquisitions.
